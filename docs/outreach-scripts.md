# Get Grub — Restaurant Outreach Scripts

Use these to sign your first venues. Lead with *their* empty tables, not your app.
Target independents first (owner-operated, you can speak to the decision-maker on
the spot). Best time to walk in: mid-afternoon (2:30–4:30pm), between services.

---

## Door-to-door / in-person (the main channel)

**Opener (15 seconds):**
> "Hi — are you the owner or manager? I'm [name], I'm local, I run Get Grub. One
> quick question: which shifts are quietest for you — early weeknights?"

*(Let them answer. You're now talking about their problem, not your product.)*

> "That's exactly what we fix. Get Grub fills those empty off-peak tables with
> new diners. You pick the times, the discount, and a cap on how many covers —
> and you switch it off the moment you're busy. It's free to join, no contract,
> and you only ever fund a discount on a table that actually gets filled."

**The hook:**
> "Most places we talk to are losing money on staff standing around at 6pm on a
> Tuesday. An empty seat earns nothing — a filled one at 30% off still covers
> the wages you're already paying. Can I set up your quietest shift right now on
> my phone? Takes two minutes and you can pull it any time."

**Close:**
> "Great — what's a slow window this week we could test? I'll get you live and
> check in Friday to show you the covers it brought in."

## Cold email / DM

**Subject:** Filling your quiet weeknight tables — [Restaurant name]

> Hi [name],
>
> I run Get Grub, a Milton Keynes app that sends diners to restaurants during
> their quiet shifts. You choose the times, the discount and a cap on covers,
> and pause it whenever you're busy.
>
> It's free to join, no contract, and you only fund a discount on tables that
> actually get filled — no percentage of your bill, no hidden fees to your
> guests (they pay one flat £1.50 booking fee).
>
> Could I pop in this week and set up your quietest shift as a free test? Two
> minutes and you can switch it off any time.
>
> [name] · partners@getgrub.app

Keep it under 120 words. Follow up once after 3 days.

---

## Objection handling

**"I don't want to discount / cheapen the brand."**
> "Totally fair — that's why you only discount the shifts *you* pick, capped to a
> few covers, and the payment's discreet in-app. It's not a permanent 2-for-1
> banner; it's a lever you pull on a dead Tuesday and switch off Friday night."

**"I tried [Groupon/Tastecard] and it was full of bargain-hunters who never
returned."**
> "Those flood you with blanket discounts and give you no control or data. Get
> Grub caps covers per slot, and diners earn loyalty credit to come back — and
> you'll see your repeat-visit rate in the dashboard."

**"What does it cost / what's the catch?"**
> "Free to list, no contract, no monthly fee in the pilot. We make our money from
> a small flat booking fee the *diner* pays — never a cut of your bill."

**"What if I get slammed / can't handle the covers?"**
> "You set the cap — say 10 covers for that window — and once it's full it stops.
> If service gets busy you pause it with one tap and it disappears from the app."

**"I'm too busy to manage another system."**
> "It's two minutes to set a recurring slot, and redeeming is just typing a code
> and the bill total. I'll set up your first one now and check in Friday."

**"Send me some info."**
> "Of course — but it's genuinely faster to just switch one quiet shift on and
> see real covers by the weekend. Shall we do that and I'll email the details
> too?" *(Always try to leave with one live slot.)*

---

## After you sign them
- Set up one recurring off-peak slot before you leave.
- Add them on the dashboard, take a couple of food photos for their listing.
- Book a 5-minute Friday check-in to show covers filled — that's what earns the
  second, better slot and the referral to their neighbour.
