# Get Grub — Milton Keynes Pilot Plan

A focused plan to prove the model in one city before spending on growth. The
doctrine from the research: **supply first, one city, density before demand.**

---

## Why Milton Keynes
- Underserved: EatClub (London/Manchester/Bristol/Leeds/Liverpool) and NeoTaste
  (6 UK cities) are **not** here. You can own it.
- Compact, car-friendly, dense centre (CMK) with a cluster of independents +
  chains around Midsummer/Avebury Boulevard and the Hub.
- It's home turf — you can do door-to-door yourself.

## The one number that matters at launch
**Restaurant supply.** Marketplaces die on empty supply, not demand. Get to
**~25–40 active venues in CMK** before any real diner marketing. (Big-city
launches run 150–450; a single town core needs far fewer to feel "full".)

---

## Phase 1 — Supply (weeks 1–4)
**Goal: 25–40 venues live with at least one recurring off-peak slot each.**

- Walk CMK with the [sales sheet](restaurant-sales-sheet.md) and
  [scripts](outreach-scripts.md). Target 10–15 doors/day, aim to sign 2–4.
- Prioritise owner-operated independents (decision-maker on site).
- Leave every signed venue with **one live recurring slot** and food photos.
- Friday check-ins: show covers filled, upgrade their slot, ask for one referral.
- Track in a simple sheet: venue, contact, status, slot live (y/n), covers so far.

**Exit criteria:** 25+ venues live, each with ≥1 recurring slot.

## Phase 2 — Demand (weeks 4–8)
**Goal: enough diners that slots actually fill, proving the loop.**

In rough order of cost-effectiveness:
1. **Local press / listicles** — pitch "new MK app for restaurant deals" to
   MKFM, MK Citizen, local Instagram food pages. Free, high-trust.
2. **TikTok/Instagram food creators** in MK — pay micro-creators to film a real
   booking + meal ("hidden gem deals in MK"). Dominant discovery format.
3. **Student channel** — MK College / Cranfield nearby; Save the Student-style
   posts, a campus ambassador.
4. **Referral** — give £5 credit / get £5 after the friend's first redemption.
5. **Launch-week subsidy (optional, surgical)** — you fund a few headline 50%
   slots for buzz; don't make it a habit.

**Exit criteria:** slots regularly selling out; repeat bookings appearing.

## Phase 3 — Prove it (weeks 8–12)
**Goal: a results story you can show the next 100 venues and any investor.**

- Pull from the dashboard: covers filled, revenue delivered to venues, repeat
  rate, slot fill %.
- Get 3–5 short owner testimonials ("filled 14 covers on a dead Tuesday").
- That case study is your sales weapon for scaling supply and your seed-raise
  proof point.

## What to measure (weekly)
| Metric | Why it matters | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Active venues (≥1 live slot) | Supply density | Dashboard / your sheet |
| Slot fill rate | Is demand meeting supply? | Slots page |
| Covers filled | Core value delivered | Overview |
| Revenue delivered to venues | The venue ROI story | Overview |
| Diner repeat rate | Retention / loyalty working | Bookings |
| Booking-fee revenue | Your unit economics | claims × £1.50 |

## Money model during the pilot
- **Venues:** free (no commission, no monthly fee). Removes all sales friction.
- **Diners:** flat £1.50 booking fee — revenue-positive from booking #1.
- **Later (post-pilot):** introduce a per-cover success fee and/or a £49–99/mo
  premium dashboard tier; consider a diner subscription that waives the fee.
  (See the monetization section of the competitive playbook.)

## Avoid the known traps
- **Wriggle's trap:** venues quit when they get busy. Our slots are off-peak-only
  and pausable, so they stay listed year-round — keep reminding owners of this.
- **TheFork's trap:** don't buy diner demand at big-city CAC before supply is
  dense and the booking fee covers acquisition. Stay frugal until Phase 2 proves
  fill.
