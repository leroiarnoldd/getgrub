# Get Grub — Restaurant Sales Sheet

*One page. Hand this to an owner or manager, or use it as your talking points.*

---

## Fill your empty tables. Pay only when it works.

Get Grub sends diners to your restaurant **during your quiet times** — the
Tuesday 6pm tables that would otherwise sit empty. You decide the times, the
discount, and exactly how many covers. Turn it off the second you get busy.

An empty seat earns nothing. At 3–5% margins, a filled off-peak cover at 30%
off is almost pure contribution — covering staff you're already paying for.

---

## How it works

1. **You set the offer** — "fill 12 covers, Tue–Thu 5–6:30pm, 30% off."
2. **Diners book that exact slot** in the app (capacity-capped — you never get
   swamped).
3. **They show a code; you redeem it** in your dashboard and enter the bill.
4. **You see the numbers** — covers filled, revenue delivered, repeat visits.

You stay in control: pause or close any slot instantly when you're full.
